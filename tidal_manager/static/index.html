📖 User Guide & Features

v1.2
🖱️

Drag & Drop (Reordering & Transfers)

In-Playlist Reordering: Grab any track by the grip handle (⋮⋮) on the left and drag up or down. A cyan indicator line shows the target position. Your new order is immediately synced to TIDAL in the cloud!

Cross-Panel Transfers: Drag songs from Panel A to Panel B (or vice versa) to copy them. Hold Alt or Shift while dropping to move them.

📊

Sort & Save Order

Sort tracks dynamically by Title, Artist, Album, or Duration (ascending or descending). Click "Save Order" to permanently save this sequence to your cloud playlist on TIDAL.

🔍

Duplicate Finder & 1-Click Cleaner

Click "Duplicates" in any playlist header to automatically scan for duplicate tracks (matching exact TIDAL IDs and normalized title & artist). Click "Clean Duplicates" to remove copies while safely preserving the original track.

💾

Backup, Export & Import

Export: Save your playlists as JSON (complete metadata), CSV (spreadsheet & DJ tool format), or M3U8 (media players).

Import: Upload backup files — choose to create a new playlist or append tracks directly to Panel A or Panel B.

⚡

Auto-Split (> 99 Tracks)

Designed for car head-units and audio gear with a 99-track limit: automatically splits large playlists into numbered chunks ([Name] 1, [Name] 2, [Name] 3...).

🔀

Merge & Cloud Shuffle

Merge: Combine multiple playlists into a new unified master playlist (with optional automatic deduplication).

Shuffle: Permanently randomize track ordering directly in the TIDAL cloud.

🔎

TIDAL Catalog Search & Audio Preview

Search: Press Cmd+K (or Ctrl+K) to search millions of TIDAL tracks and add them directly to Panel A or Panel B with 1 click.

Audio Preview: Click the album artwork of any song to play a 30-second audio preview in the bottom player bar.

⌨️

Keyboard Shortcuts

Cmd+K / Ctrl+K: Open TIDAL catalog search

Esc: Close active modal dialog
